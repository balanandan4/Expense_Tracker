Problem Statement

Managing personal finances manually is difficult and time-consuming.

Many individuals struggle to manage daily expenses because they rely on notebooks or spreadsheets that are difficult to maintain and analyze. Without proper budgeting, people often overspend and fail to reach their financial goals.

Users also find it hard to identify unnecessary expenses or understand exactly where their money goes every month.

Expense Tracker with Budget Analysis solves this with an easy-to-use digital platform that records transactions, categorizes expenses, compares actual spending against planned budgets, and generates detailed financial reports.
